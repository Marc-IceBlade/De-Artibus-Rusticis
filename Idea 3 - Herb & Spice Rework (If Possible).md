- Herbs/Spices give roughly a 1.2x multiplier when used in the cooking pot
- Maybe we can add a stone mortar and pestle, which is a block that you can interact with? It would have 4 input slots and one output slot.
- If you fill the slots with different herbs/spices, you can combine them into a higher grade of spice blend, which will only take up one slot in the cooking pot, so i.e:
- 2 slots of either herb or spice -> regular spice blend 1.4x
- 3 slots of either herb or spice –> rich spice blend 1.6x
- 4 slots of either herb or spice -> premium spice blend 1.8x
